# Brainstorming Lite

Use a short discovery loop to reduce ambiguity without forcing a formal spec.

## Pattern

1. State the real decision to make.
2. Identify 2-3 viable options and the tradeoff that matters.
3. Recommend one option when evidence is enough.
4. Ask only blocking questions.
5. Convert the result into implementation constraints or a short plan.

## Anti-Triggers

- Do not use for clone, status, formatting, simple config, or obvious bug fixes.
- Do not require a design document unless the user asks for one.
- Do not continue brainstorming after the implementation boundary is clear.
- Do not use as a mandatory pre-step for every feature.
